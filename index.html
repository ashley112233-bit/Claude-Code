Our story

How C J Jewellery began

C J Jewellery began when Celia received a bracelet-making kit and started creating pieces as a hobby. Family and friends loved what she made and began asking for bracelets of their own. What started as a pastime soon grew into a small word-of-mouth business.

Celia enjoys creating jewellery that brings pleasure to the people who wear it. Each piece receives personal care, whether it is colourful and playful or simple and elegant.

Privacy

The enquiry form prepares an email on your own device using your own email application. It does not send anything automatically.

This website does not store the information you type into the form. Nothing is saved on a server or database.

If you choose to send the prepared email, the information in it will be used by C J Jewellery to reply to your enquiry and to discuss a possible order with you.

Please do not include any unnecessary sensitive information in your enquiry.

This is a small, handmade business. This page explains our approach in plain English, but it is not a formal claim of compliance with data protection law such as UK GDPR.

Accessibility
